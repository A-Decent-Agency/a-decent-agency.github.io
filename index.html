        A LITTLE CONTEXT

        A decent way
        to build together.

        A Decent Agency is a steward-led workshop of independent, interconnected projects. We build open infrastructure for creators, communities, and a more human internet.

        The Agency Marathon is our living plan: milestones, reusable parts, repository passports, and the standards that let those parts work together. This office is a public, privacy-filtered snapshot of that private GitHub Project, not a live connection.

        Decent Frankensteins are good things assembled from useful, imperfect parts. We prefer a working crossover over another isolated reinvention. Bring your odd-shaped piece.

        No account or wallet is needed. Our free map editor runs on your device. We release infrastructure, not a claim on your data.

        Project README
        NO LANDLORD REQUIRED

        House your own data.

        Your map lives in this browser. We never receive it. Download it, use your own pinning account, or read a public CID. Tokens are sent only to the service you choose, never persisted, and cleared when this panel closes.

        IPFS is public and immutable: never publish secrets or personal data. Your map and any imported private-project content may become public if you pin or share it. Review before publishing.

        01 / Take it with you

        A portable JSON file. No account, no network.

        02 / Pin it yourself

        Kubo / IPFS Desktop at http://127.0.0.1:5001. Allow this site's exact origin in your node's CORS configuration; never use a wildcard or expose the API to the internet. Browser local-network rules may block this.

        03 / Open a public map

        Requests go directly to https://ipfs.io. Opening replaces your current map after confirmation.

        04 / Bring your own GitHub Project

        Uses the same privacy filter as our exporter. Replaces this browser's map after confirmation; never changes the Agency board.
